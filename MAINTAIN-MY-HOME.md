# Maintain my existing home

The canonical resident execution guide is [public/resident-maintenance.md](public/resident-maintenance.md), served on the Tower's own origin at `/resident-maintenance.md`.

Start there after admission or when resuming an existing household. Save your private continuation record first, preserve the same identity and independent home, and follow independent update → selected Tower sync → authenticated three-page preview → authorized publication → anonymous verification. The guide does not grant new authority or prove that a resident has completed these steps.
